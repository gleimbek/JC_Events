JC EventsIt was founded in 2012 with a clear mission: to transform every celebration into a unique, elegant, and unforgettable experience. What began as a small family business is today one of the most renowned event production companies in the region.

We specialize in weddings, quinceañeras, graduations, baby showers, and high-end catering. Every event bears our signature of excellence, creativity, and attention to detail—qualities that set us apart.

Journey

Our
History

Each year has been another step toward excellence. Discover the moments that have shaped us as a company

2012
The Beginning

JC Events began as a family business with the organization of our first intimate wedding for 80 guests, marking the start of a remarkable journey.

2015
Service Expansion

We incorporated professional catering services and expanded our offerings to include Quinceañeras and graduations, tripling our client base.

2018
Award for Excellence

We were recognized as the Best Event Planning Company in the region, solidifying our reputation and opening up new opportunities.

2021
Digital Renovation

We modernized our digital presence and adopted new technologies for online quotes and personalized tracking of every event.

2024
500+ Events and Counting

We surpassed the 500-event milestone, maintaining our status as a benchmark for quality and elegance in event planning.
